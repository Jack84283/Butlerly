# Butlerly issuer logo sources

This folder is bundled with Butlerly for **offline** display. Logos identify the
selected institution; they do not imply any banking integration, endorsement,
affiliation, or account connection. Institution names and marks remain the
trademarks of their respective owners. Do not infer a bank from a similar mark.
