2. The local-first model
Butlerly Alpha is designed to keep its core personal-finance records on the device in an app-controlled local database and private evidence storage. Core finance operations are intended to work offline without a Butlerly account. Data stored on a device can nevertheless be accessible to its operating system, device backup services or anyone with access to the device. Butlerly's local-first design does not mean absolute secrecy, guaranteed recovery, or a blanket assertion that no data is ever processed outside the device.

The approved Alpha does not include built-in cloud-account linking, automatic background synchronization, remote document retrieval or online assistant/help search. The release engineer must verify that no unintended SDK, OCR, AI, currency-rate, analytics, diagnostics or networking path changes these statements in the actual binary.

3. Information you choose to process
Depending on the features you use, information may include transaction amounts, currencies, dates, directions and descriptions; merchant and category names; tags and notes; payment-source names, balances or identifiers entered by you; imported bank or card statements, CSV files, receipts, photos and documents; recognized text and original evidence; duplicate and reconciliation decisions; budgets and rules; locally derived reports and insights; preferences; Financial Profile Survey answers; backups and exports.

These records may be sensitive and may include names, addresses or account identifiers present in documents you select. Survey responses are self-reported context, not verified bank data or suitability assessments. Butlerly does not require you to provide bank login credentials for the approved Alpha workflow. Do not import data about other people unless authorized.

4. Why the App uses this information
The App processes records to let you enter, display, amend, search, review, compare, classify, reconcile, import, export, back up, restore and delete financial information. Local rules and calculations may derive totals, trends, likely matches or suggestions; derived outputs can contain errors and should not silently replace original evidence. Preferences and optional survey responses support available in-app experiences. Each of these purposes depends on your actions and the implemented features.

5. When information can leave the App
You may choose to export or share files, save a backup, attach content through operating-system selectors, or contact support outside the App. A file you independently store in iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or another service is governed by that service's terms and privacy practices. Downloading a document from such a provider or a bank occurs outside Butlerly; you then select a locally available supported file to import. Butlerly does not automatically connect to that account or keep the file synchronized in the approved Alpha.

OS-level app distribution, device cloud backup, keyboard/camera services, crash reporting controlled by a store or operating system, or other system functions may involve separate providers. Which recipients actually receive App data, for what purpose, and on which platforms must be verified for the release. The current draft does not assert that there is zero collection, zero sharing or zero diagnostics.

6. Camera, photos, files and recognition
If you invoke import or capture, Butlerly may request access through the camera, photo library or file picker. Platform permission settings control the access afforded to the App. Receipts and statements may contain sensitive financial or identity information. On-device OCR and deterministic extraction are distinct from remote AI services. The approved Alpha does not authorize uploading financial documents or prompts to external OCR/AI providers; the shipped engine and fallback behavior must be audited before publishing a categorical local-only claim.

7. No authorized cloud sync or behavioral advertising
The intended Alpha has no Butlerly-operated accounts, cloud data syncing, remote financial profiling, paid entitlements, marketing tracking or sale of user finance records. This is a product restriction and not independent proof that every included SDK is free of network activity. Before release, audit network traffic, third-party SDKs, permission manifests, data-safety declarations and any diagnostics; if any actual recipient exists, update the policy to identify recipient categories, purposes, retention and consent or legal basis. Do not advertise an unverified “we never collect anything” guarantee.

8. Retention, deletion, exports and backups
On-device data is generally retained until you edit, archive, erase, uninstall or otherwise remove it through available controls or device operations. Archiving is not the same as permanent deletion. Restoring a backup can reintroduce records; “Merge” and “Replace” have different consequences. Evidence files and database records may be stored separately. Deleted local content can remain in independent exports, backups, device copies or external services you selected. The App cannot delete copies beyond its control.

Where a verified external service receives personal data, the final policy must state who holds it, the retention period or criteria, and a verified request route for access, correction, deletion and other applicable rights. Without a Butlerly account in the approved Alpha, requests to delete a central Butlerly finance account do not apply; local erasure still requires user action.

9. Security and recovery limitations
The design uses local storage and app-controlled evidence files and supports encrypted portable backups where implemented. Protection also depends on device locks, OS security, the storage destination and your password practices. No product guarantees prevention of unauthorized access, accidental loss, corruption or unsuccessful recovery. Do not send passwords, backup keys or complete financial archives in ordinary support messages.

10. Your choices and rights
You can choose what to enter/import, review detected transactions, correct records, and use supported backup/export/erase controls. You may manage camera/photo/file permissions in the operating system. Rights and response deadlines under privacy law depend on the actual controller, jurisdiction and nature of processing.
