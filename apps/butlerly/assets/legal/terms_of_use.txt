2. What Butlerly does
The Alpha is a local-first personal-finance application for entering and reviewing transactions; organizing merchants, categories and payment sources; importing supported downloaded files and captured receipts; reviewing possible duplicates and statement rows; managing settlements and budgets; and viewing financial summaries, insights and an optional Financial Profile Survey. Feature availability depends on the actual Alpha build and device. Survey answers are information you provide, not verified financial transactions or professional assessments.

Core Alpha operation is intended to work without a Butlerly account, cloud login or continuous network access. The approved Alpha does not include direct cloud-account connections, automatic synchronization, remote document retrieval, external AI/OCR processing, or purchase-based Pro/Family entitlements. Obtain a supported document outside the App and deliberately select the locally available file for import. A future feature is not a present service or entitlement.
