LEG-0001 | English

These Terms explain the use of Butlerly for organizing personal financial information. Available features may vary with your installed version and device.

1. What Butlerly is
Butlerly is a local-first personal-finance organizer for transactions, receipts, statements, payments, budgets and analysis. You may choose to import supported files that you first obtain outside the App. Butlerly does not require an account or provide built-in cloud synchronization or automatic downloads from banks or storage providers. Available features depend on the installed build and device.

2. Your records and decisions
You decide what to enter or import and must have permission to use the information. Check extracted text, amounts, dates, currencies, duplicate suggestions, settlement entries and reports before relying on them. Butlerly does not verify bank balances, source documents or survey answers independently. Automated results may be wrong.

3. Files, backups and recovery
Core records are designed to remain on your device. If you choose to share or export a file, copies may exist outside Butlerly. Export to file produces readable, unencrypted information and is for review, not re-import into Butlerly. Portable backups use their own encrypted backup workflow. Keep passwords and exported files secure; deleting app data does not erase copies you have stored elsewhere. Backup and Restore (Merge or Replace) can affect records; review confirmations and check recovery.
